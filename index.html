    How to order

    Choose

    Pick your deras and add them to your cart.

    Pay

    In Kenya, pay by M-Pesa and enter the code. Elsewhere, we confirm payment options with you on WhatsApp.

    We confirm

    Your order reaches us and WhatsApp. We confirm payment and pack your dera.

    Delivery

    Bolt or Uber within Kenya. International shipping is arranged with you personally.

    Questions

    Do you ship internationally?

    International orders are welcome. Place your order and we confirm shipping cost, delivery time and payment options with you on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

    What is a Nursing Dera?

    A dera designed for easy breastfeeding access, comfortable and flowing. Ask us on WhatsApp about current nursing pieces.

    How much is delivery in Kenya?

    We deliver by Bolt or Uber. The ride fee depends on your location and is paid by you. We confirm it with you on WhatsApp.

    How do I pay?

    In Kenya, pay the total by M-Pesa to the number shown in your cart, then enter the confirmation code. International customers: we confirm payment options on WhatsApp.

    What size are the deras?

    Our deras are loose and free-flowing and suit most sizes. Message us for measurements of any piece.

    What do Reserved and Sold mean?

    Reserved means another customer is buying that dera. Sold means it has gone. Each dye pattern is unique, so message us if you would like something similar.

    Is the dera exactly like the photo?

    Yes, the photos show the actual piece. Each dye pattern is unique, so once a design sells out it will not return in the same pattern.
