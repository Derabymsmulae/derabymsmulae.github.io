    Delivery and payment

    Simple, clear and stress free from cart to doorstep.

    Pay by M-Pesa (Kenya)

    Send the total to our M-Pesa number shown in your cart, then enter the confirmation code when you place your order.

    Bolt or Uber delivery

    Within Kenya we book the ride for you. The ride fee depends on your location and is paid by you.

    International orders

    We confirm shipping cost, delivery time and payment options with you on WhatsApp before you pay anything.

    Reserved and Sold

    Reserved means another customer is buying that dera. Sold means it has gone. Each dye pattern is unique.
